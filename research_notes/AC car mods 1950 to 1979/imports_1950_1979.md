# AC1 car mods: 1950-1979 import cars sold in the US

Research date 2026-10-03. Nothing downloaded. None of the cars in `_installed_cars.txt` is an import, so nothing was skipped for that reason (the AC 289 / Cobra entries there are American-badged and excluded on purpose).

Method caveat, read first: Patreon, assettoworld.com and assettocorsamods.io returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher, so many rows below come from search snippets or re-host listings only. Re-host sites (assettoworld, assettocorsamods.io, modslab, modland, assettohub, assettomods.com) rarely name the author and sometimes carry leaked Patreon or decrypted mods. Rows marked VERIFIED were read on the original page. Overtake.gg also hosts GT Legends, rFactor 2 and Automobilista mods, and several "AC" hits turned out to be other sims (flagged below).

## Q1. Best findable mod per car (street spec preferred)

### Takeaway
Street-spec, original-model, free, unencrypted AC1 mods of 1950-79 imports are rare. Only a handful are verified as such: Datsun Fairlady 2000 roadster (Alguecool & Pasta2000, explicitly free+unencrypted, sharing allowed with credit), Austin Mini Cooper S 1275 '64 (Pessio), Alfa Romeo Giulietta Spider (|Viper|), The Pantera Project (OldRebel, on smallblockhero's base), VW Beetle 1600s and Porsche 914 (assettocorsa.club / Dmitrii Alekseev; the 914 model is a Forza rip). Most other cars exist only as race/drift builds or anonymous re-hosts.

### Inferences
- Shortlist that fits Street Corsa's rules (street spec, unencrypted, original model, open terms): Datsun Fairlady 2000 (best: explicit free/unencrypted/shareable statement), then Pessio Mini, |Viper| Giulietta Spider, The Pantera Project (but the base model's own terms are unknown). Beetle 1600s is usable for private play; Porsche 914 is a Forza rip and must stay out of any shipped/open-source content.
- Bazza's THR Touring Car Legends packs cover many US-sold imports (2002, 510, RX3, 911, Mini, MGA, MGB GT, Austin-Healey 3000, Jaguar Mk2, 2000GT) with real-data physics but in race trim; detuning them to street spec is a plausible route, subject to permission.
- Many re-hosted "street" listings show tuned power figures (240Z 315 bhp, 510 wagon 351 bhp), so expect to rewrite physics for stock street use.
- The game's encrypted-mod check matters here: EX-MODS cars (P1800, Ghibli) and xCHx_Exitus (250 Lusso) are known or implied to be encrypted.

## Q3. Mod teams and authors with 1950-79 import catalogues; licence situation

### Takeaway
The deepest 1950-79 import catalogues are race packs: AC Legends (Smallblock Hero and Bazza, hosted by Tarnhörner Racing / thracing.de) and Bazza's Touring Car Legends. Street-car specialists are scattered single authors (Pessio, |Viper|, Alguecool, Dmitrii Alekseev on assettocorsa.club, Velo, Navi Street Cars). Almost no AC car mod carries an open licence; the only explicit free/unencrypted/share-with-credit statement found was on the Datsun Fairlady 2000.

### Cited Findings
- AC Legends: modular historic GT platform by Smallblock Hero and Bazza covering 1960s-70s sports and touring cars — [THR summary via search](https://thracing.de/ac-legends-gt-classics-by-bazza/). GT Classics (70s GT: BMW CSL, Ferrari 365 GTB Daytona, Ford Capri RS3100, Porsche 906, Nissan Skyline GT-R, Pantera Gr4, Porsche RSR 73/74, Broadspeed Jaguar), v3.0 Nov 2020, MediaFire download, no licence statement — [THR](https://thracing.de/ac-legends-gt-classics-by-bazza/), [DrivingItalia](https://www.drivingitalia.net/forums/topic/78229-ac-legends-mod-gt-legends-in-assetto-corsa/). The team got explicit permission from Pessio to use his Pantera Gr4 model; DrDoomslab remastered the models — [DrivingItalia](https://www.drivingitalia.net/forums/topic/78229-ac-legends-mod-gt-legends-in-assetto-corsa/)
- AC Legends GTC 60s expanded to 21 cars on 2025-01-07 (added Abarth Simca 2000GT, Alfa Giulia TZ2, Ferrari 250 GT SWB, Ferrari 275 GTB Competizione, TVR Griffith 200, Porsche 911 SWB (R), Corvette SCCA); WSC Legends 60s to 24 cars; each pack ships a PDF with credits for models, physics, skins and sounds; needs CSP and some DLC — [ORD](https://www.onlineracedriver.com/2025/01/07/ac-legends-gtc-60s-and-wsc-legends-mods-get-updated/). GTC 60s current page lists v1.3.2 — [THR](https://thracing.de/ac-legends-gtc-60s-by-bazza/)
- Touring Car Legends by Bazza (v1.0, 2019-11-26, free, 18 cars): 60s pack Austin-Healey 3000, Lotus Cortina, Ford Fairlane, Ford Mustang, Hillman Imp, Jaguar Mk2, MGA, MGB GT, Mini Cooper, Toyota 2000GT; 70s pack Porsche 911 RS 2.7, Alfa GTA, Alpine A110, AMG 300SEL, BMW 2002, Datsun 510, Ford Escort, Mazda RX3; physics built from real data; no licence statement on the page — [THR](https://thracing.de/touring-car-legends/)
- THR also hosts DRM Revival (German 70s-80s Group 5, incl. BMW 2002 Turbo and 320 Turbo) — [THR](https://thracing.de/), [bsimracing](https://www.bsimracing.com/drm-revival-mod-for-assetto-corsa-first-bmw-2002-group-5-preview/)
- Historic Sim Studios: Patreon-funded, every completed mod released free to the public; free cars include a 1968 Daihatsu P-5 (race prototype, never sold in the US) — [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/historicsimstudios), [site](https://historicsimstudios.com/)
- EX-MODS (Patreon, "premium quality", various eras; Volvo P1800S, Maserati Ghibli; encrypted per the "decrypted" re-host) — [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/cw/ex_mods), [modland](https://www.modland.net/assetto-corsa-mods/cars/volvo-p1800s-decrypted.html)
- assettocorsa.club: curated free mods "all tested" (Beetle 1600s, Porsche 914, Mini Cooper S, 300 SEL 6.3); its 914 credits a Forza model — [site](https://assettocorsa.club/), [914](https://assettocorsa.club/mods/auto/porsche-914.html)
- ACTK / assetto-fr.com: updates and decrypts of other authors' cars (P1800S) — [ACTK garage](https://www.assetto-fr.com/garage/volvo/)
- Encryption: community view is that many encrypted mods outside the big teams use models stolen from other games (e.g. Gran Turismo); encrypted kn5 is tied to data.acd, so editing physics breaks it — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/244210/discussions/0/6142387507324477014/)
- Conversions from other games must respect the originals' licences, many game companies forbid them — [AC Supply FAQ](https://www.acsupply.cx/faq)
- Many British/German classics on Overtake are GT Legends conversions (Spridget by Jon c, sunalp2's E-Type and Healey 100S, InsaneDruid's Elan); Team21's GTL RX-3 explicitly forbids conversion — [Overtake Spridget](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/spridget-mod-mg-midgets-mk1-and-sprite-mk2-v1-0.21800/), [Team21 RX-3](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/mazda-rx-3-v1_1-by-team21.21917/)
- Teams the brief named (VRC, RSS, URD) are modern-car teams whose full-scratch work is encrypted — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/244210/discussions/0/6142387507324477014/). Legion appears only as editor of WKMOD's RX-3 — [assetto-db](https://assetto-db.com/car/mazda_rx3_wkmod). No 1950-79 import catalogues were found for "Tando Buddies", "WDTS", "Reboot Team", "Kyuubi", "Pyyer" or "GTP".

### Inferences
- For an open-source game, the safe default is: do not ship any third-party car; let players install mods themselves. Where shipping is wanted, ask authors directly; the Fairlady 2000 author has already allowed sharing with credit, and Pessio has a record of granting model permission (to AC Legends).
- Race packs from Bazza/AC Legends are the richest source of correct-era imports with real-data physics, and the PDF credits make asking for permission straightforward.

### Gaps
- No AC car mod with a formal open licence (CC, MIT, etc.) was found.
- Licence text for the THR packs, Pessio's Mini, the Giulietta Spider and The Pantera Project was not stated on the pages read; the PDFs that ship with the packs (credits) were not read.
- Overtake.gg's own resource redistribution terms were not checked.
