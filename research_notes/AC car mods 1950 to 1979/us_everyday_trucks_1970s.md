# AC1 car mods: 1970-1979 American non-muscle cars (full-size, luxury, wagons, police/taxi, trucks/vans/4x4, economy, customs)

Scope note: research done 2026-10-03 by web search only (nothing downloaded). Many of the big mod aggregators (assettoworld.com, modland.net, assettocorsamods.io, Patreon post pages) returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool, so a lot of detail below comes from search snippets, ACTK (assetto-fr.com) and Assettoland garage lists, modslab.net, acmods.net and Overtake.gg. Overtake.gg (formerly RaceDepartment) search for caprice / cadillac / torino / c10 returned **no** car mods in this group: this class of car lives almost entirely on Patreon, YouTube descriptions and re-upload aggregators.

Already installed and therefore not re-listed (from `_installed_cars.txt`): `dodge_monaco` + `dodge_monaco_police` (1974 Monaco), `chevrolet_el_camino_70_mby` and the `chevy_elcamino_1970_*` set, `mercury_montego_1970`, `rpm_chevy_s2_1973/1974` (Argentine Chevy Serie 2, Nova-based), `chevy_c10hotrod`, `lm_galaxie_500`, `lovetrain_hornet`, `1st_maverik70`, `atcc_chrysler_valiant_*` (Australian).

## Which 1970s American everyday/luxury/truck/van mods exist and where?

### Takeaway
Very few. Confirmed AC1 mods in this group number roughly a dozen: two Gran Torinos (Uncle M), a 1972 Monte Carlo and a 1972 C-10 (MacedoSTI), a 1978 Checker Marathon taxi, AMC Gremlin X (several versions) and Pacer X 1977 (smallblockhero), a 1976 Jeep CJ5 (Stereo), a 1979 Chevy LUV lowrider (LOVETRAIN), GMC Vandura variants and a few 1971 C10 / ratlook C10 trucks. No AC1 mod was found for any 1970s Cadillac, Lincoln, Buick, Oldsmobile, Chrysler, Plymouth Fury/Volaré, Dodge Aspen, Ford LTD, 1970s Impala/Caprice, 1970s F-series, Bronco (1st gen / 1978), K5 Blazer, Ranchero, Pinto, Vega or Chevette.

### Inferences
- The 1970s US everyday/luxury segment is essentially empty in AC1; the realistic catalogue is: Gran Torino x2, Monte Carlo '72, C-10 '71/'72, Checker '78, Gremlin X, Pacer X, Jeep CJ5 '76, Chevy LUV lowrider '79, GMC Vandura. Everything else (Cadillac/Lincoln/Buick/Olds/Chrysler land yachts, LTD, Caprice, square-body K-trucks, Bronco, Blazer, Ranchero, wagons) would have to be modelled by the game or community.
- Release years cluster 2023-2025 (CJ5 2023, Pacer upload 2023, Checker upload 2024, Monte Carlo Patreon ~2025, LOVETRAIN pack 2025-26).

### Gaps
- Could not open assettoworld.com, modland.net, assettocorsamods.io, Patreon post pages (403), so authorship, dates and version history for Checker, Gremlin, Vandura, Gran Torino 1974 and LUV lowrider are from snippets only.
- Could not confirm any mod for: Impala/Caprice 1971-79, Ford LTD/Galaxie 1970s, Thunderbird 1970s, Mercury Marquis, Pontiac Grand Prix/Bonneville/LeMans, Olds Delta 88/Toronado, Buick Riviera boattail/Electra/LeSabre, Cadillac Eldorado/DeVille/Seville, Lincoln Continental/Mark IV/V, Chrysler New Yorker/Cordoba, Dodge Coronet/Aspen, Plymouth Fury/Volaré, any 1970s wagon, Chevy/Ford/Dodge 1970s pickups other than the C-10, Li'l Red Express/Warlock, El Camino/Ranchero 1971-79, Ford/Dodge vans, Bronco, Blazer, Pinto, Vega, Chevette. Absence from search is not proof of absence: Patreon-only or Discord-only releases (e.g. Uncle M, MacedoSTI, smallblockhero, Redd, LOVETRAIN) are poorly indexed.
- Year of the "C10 Ratlook" and of `lm_galaxie_500` / `chevy_c10hotrod` (installed) not determined.

## Which are good quality originals, which are rips?

### Takeaway
Only the 1976 Jeep CJ5 (Stereo) is confirmed as an original model with documented physics features. The C-10 '72 is built on a bought Hum3D shell; the Gran Torino Sport is a CSR Classics rip; the Vandura and Country Squire lineage is Forza-derived; the Checker's origin is unknown.

### Cited Findings
- Jeep CJ5 1976: original creation by Stereo, CSP 0.2.0 features (transfer case on Extra A), deliberate solid-axle pull; known to roll over easily — [Overtake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/1976-jeep-cj5.65151/)
- Chevrolet Cheyenne C-10 1972: shell from Hum3D (commercial 3D model store), 3D work Lucas Macedo, physics smallblockhero; RainFX, wipers, LODs, AO — good CSP feature set — [acmods.net](https://acmods.net/cars/chevrolet-cheyenne-c-10-1972/)
- Gran Torino Sport 1972: "Original Source: CSR Classic, Improved by: DMN, Converted to AC by: Uncle M" — game rip — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4l7Gen-iT-w)
- smallblockhero's vintage mods credit 3D models from "Turn10 Forza, SimBin, Retro Expansion, Grid" — his Pacer/Gremlin may share that origin (not confirmed per car) — [xfactor forum](https://xfactorracing.com/index.php/forum/assetto-corsa/10883-assetto-corsa-vintage-car-mod-by-smallblockhero)
- Some GMC Vandura AC mods are reported as Forza conversions; base credits for the A-Team edit list Creak, Silvermanblue, PrT Audiman, Brett, CuriousMike — [search summary of A-Team mod pages](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/gmc_vandera_a-team.19885/) (credits list came from aggregated snippets, not verified on page)
- Pacer X 1977 is set to 400 hp / 670 Nm, Monte Carlo to 450/650 hp, C10 Ratlook 789 hp: these are tuned fantasy specs, not stock road cars — [modslab Pacer](https://modslab.net/en/assetto_corsa/mods/iNe2rReBf3/); [acmods Monte Carlo](https://acmods.net/cars/chevrolet-monte-carlo-assetto-corsa-mod/); [modslab C10](https://modslab.net/en/assetto_corsa/mods/sB6z4Y7zba/)

### Inferences
- For Street Corsa, the stock-ish usable ones are the CJ5, Checker, Gran Torino 1972 351 Stock, Gran Torino 1974 Standart and C-10 '72 (has a turbo option but 242 bhp base). The Pacer, Monte Carlo, C10 Ratlook and some Vanduras would need physics retuned to stock to fit the career.
- Rip origin (CSR Classics, Forza) makes the Gran Torinos and Vanduras unsuitable to ship in an open-source repo even where the files are unencrypted.

### Gaps
- Model origin unknown for: Checker Marathon, Gremlin X versions, Pacer X, Gran Torino 1974, LUV lowrider, Monte Carlo 1972.
- No physics/quality reviews found for most of these mods.

## Which allow redistribution?

### Takeaway
None of the mods found states an explicit redistribution licence. One is confirmed encrypted (MacedoSTI Monte Carlo); the Hum3D-based C-10 carries third-party commercial asset terms; rips (CSR, Forza) cannot be redistributed. Treat all as "user installs it themselves", not as bundled content.

### Cited Findings
- Monte Carlo II Custom 1972 by MacedoSTI is distributed as "(Encrypted & CSP)" — excluded by Street Corsa's no-encrypted rule — [ModsFire file](https://modsfire.com/ddHYxuL20WcjJja)
- Jeep CJ5: no licence text on the Overtake page; encryption not mentioned — [Overtake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/1976-jeep-cj5.65151/)
- Chevrolet Cheyenne C-10 1972: no licence; uses a Hum3D shell, which has its own commercial licence terms — [acmods.net](https://acmods.net/cars/chevrolet-cheyenne-c-10-1972/)
- Checker Marathon: "Original author unknown" — no one to ask for permission — [Assettoland](https://assettoland10.wixsite.com/assettoland/other)
- GMC Vandera A-Team: uploader himself unsure of the right to share — [Overtake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/gmc_vandera_a-team.19885/)
- LOVETRAIN lowrider pack: free, described as "reuploaded", no licence — [vosan.co](https://www.vosan.co/driftcarpacks/lowrider-car-pack-lovetrain-us)

### Inferences
- Best candidate to contact for permission to bundle or to list as a recommended download: Stereo (Jeep CJ5, original model, Overtake-hosted). Everything else is either a rip, encrypted, Patreon-gated or of unknown authorship.
- Since the segment is thin, Street Corsa's 1970s everyday cars likely need to come from user installs or from commissioned/community-made originals rather than a redistributable existing pool.

### Gaps
- Encryption status not verified (cannot download) for: Gran Torinos, Checker, Gremlin X, Pacer X, C-10 '72, LUV lowrider, Vanduras. ACTK/Assettoland re-uploads sometimes mark "(Encrypted)" in file names; only the Monte Carlo was seen marked.
- Whether MacedoSTI's C-10 is also encrypted (his Monte Carlo is) is unknown.
