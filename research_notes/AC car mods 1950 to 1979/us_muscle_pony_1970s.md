# AC1 car mods: 1970-1979 US muscle and pony cars

Scope: Assetto Corsa 1 only, model years 1970-79 (plus 1980 where it is the same body). Cars already installed (see `_installed_cars.txt`) are left out unless a newer or different version exists. Installed and in scope: `1st_maverik70`, `70_ford_mustang_boss`, `ford_mustang_mach1_70(_428)`, `ford_mustang_boss_302/429`, `mby_ford_mustang_boss_429`, `mercury_cougar_1970`, `mercury_montego_1970`, `chevy_chevelle1970`, `pb_chevy_chevelle_ss_1970`, `chevrolet_el_camino_70_mby`, `chevy_elcamino_1970*`, `chevrolet_corvette1970`, `chevrolet_corvette_c3_zr1`, `gd_chevy_vette_c3_zr1`, `pontiac_firebird_468_70`, `buick_gsx`, `tmw_442_hurst`, `dodge_challenger_70`, `dodge_challenger_rt_hemi_1970`, `dodge_challenger70s_redd_tuned`, `dodge_70_bee`, `70_dodge_superbee_rm_redd_tuned`, `dodge_charger_rt_440_magnum` (1969), `dodge_char_70`, `mclk_dodgech_hrt70`, `71_plymouth_cuda_528hemi`, `71_plymouth_gtx_426hemi`, `pb_roadrunner_70`, `plymouth_supercuda`, `amc_javelin_1970(_se)`, `lovetrain_hornet(_drift)` (the AMC Hornet folder, year not checked), `rpm_chevy_s2_1973/1974` (Argentine Chevy Serie 2, not a US car).

Research method: web search plus fetches of the catalogue sites (assetto-db.com, assetto-fr.com "Garage ACTK", Assettoland, Overtake.gg). AssettoWorld and ModLand returned HTTP 403, so the facts from those sites come only from search snippets. Nothing was downloaded, so I could not check whether any KN5 is encrypted. A mod is called "not encrypted" below only when a source says so. Otherwise encryption is listed as unknown.

## Which 1970-79 American muscle/pony car mods exist and where?

### Takeaway
The 1970-72 muscle cars are well covered, but nearly all of it comes from a few old community teams: Team SmallBlockHero (SBH) and its many re-edits, Uncle M, GeroDa74, PedroBLR and AC Legends. The 1973-79 era is thin. There is a 1977 Trans Am, a 1979 Camaro Z28, a 1972 Camaro Z28, a 1972/74 Gran Torino, a 1978 Mustang II King Cobra, a 1971 Vega, a Gremlin X and a few Corvette C3 race cars. I found no AC1 mod for: the Mustang II other than the King Cobra, Pinto V8, Cyclone, the 1971-79 Cougar, the 1971+ Mach 1 apart from a 429 CJ, the 1970s Cutlass/442, Buick GS/Regal, the 1970s Nova, Duster, Dart, 1972-74 Barracuda, Matador street car, or Javelin AMX 1971-74 street car.

### Inferences
- If Street Corsa wants street cars from 1973 on, the realistic candidates are: Trans Am 1977 (SBH or GeroDa74), Camaro Z28 1979 (SBH), Mustang II King Cobra 1978 (Uncle M), Gran Torino 1972/74 (Uncle M), Corvette C3 Bell's 1980, Vega 1971 and Gremlin X. Everything else from 1973-79 would have to be modelled or come later.
- The best addition to the 1970-72 set is probably the Demon 340, Superbee 1971, Camaro Z28 1972, Mach 1 1971 and Monte Carlo 1972. They fill body styles the installed cars lack.

### Gaps
- AssettoWorld and ModLand blocked fetching (403), so author, date, CSP and encryption details for several entries come only from search snippets or are missing.
- I did not search Patreon/Ko-fi/Gumroad payware catalogues systematically (examples: GeroDa74's Patreon, the Monte Carlo Patreon post). Paid 1970s cars may exist that public indexes don't show.
- Discord-only releases and the r/assettocorsa threads were not covered.

## Which are high quality originals vs. rips?

### Takeaway
Almost nothing in this group is a documented original high-quality model. Most mods come from the SBH family of "conversions" from other sims or games, Uncle M "2in1" releases, or GeroDa74 payware, which the community says uses Forza/NFS rips. The one documented original I found is Joshua Widder's 1970 Javelin, and the author calls it unfinished.

### Cited Findings
- The AC Legends TransAm release notes credit "Conversions/ 3d Modelling: SmallBlock Hero, mickstix, Uncle M". They warn "Due to various sources the model quality may vary per car", and sounds come from "Kunos, various unknown sources". So the SBH-lineage models (and Uncle M's) are at least partly conversions of unstated origin. — [TransAm Legends v2.0 PDF](https://thracing.de/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/2020-03-26-AC-TransAm-Legends-v2.0-Release-Notes-1.pdf)
- A 2021 Overtake thread on GeroDa74's payware: users say his mods are "ripped models (from Forza, Need For Speed etc) using physics ripped from Kunos cars". One model was shown to bear "a pretty strong resemblance to the same car in Forza", and users advised against buying. — [Overtake thread](https://www.overtake.gg/threads/how-to-get-geroda74s-mods.204958/). This applies to the GeroDa74 Trans Am 1977, AAR Cuda and Greenwood C3 (my inference that the claim extends to these cars; it is not verified per car).
- The Brickyard Legends Camaro II is a GT Legends conversion that credits many earlier GTL modders. — [Overtake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/chevrolet-camaro-ii-imsa-transam-etcc-by-brickyard-legends-team.21998/)
- The 1970 AMC Javelins are original work: "3D Modeller, Physics, Textures - Joshua Widder", but the author calls it "unfinished, but playable". — [Overtake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/1970-amc-javelins.46012/)
- The SBH 1977 Trans Am (2015) is old: 4-speed manual, working gauges, animations and lights, model origin not stated. — [The Sim Review](http://thesimreview.blogspot.com/2015/01/assetto-corsa-mods-1977-trans-am-bandit.html)
- Re-edits on Assettoland are flagged clearly: they mark which mods are "ENCRYPTED" or "LOCKED" (for example the Charger 1969 by Ilya Dobrovolsky and by Nabaoui/SMPS, both ENCRYPTED & CSP; the Charger RT 426 Hellephant 1968 is LOCKED). None of the 1970-79 Dodges there carries that mark. — [Assettoland Dodge](https://assettoland10.wixsite.com/assettoland/dodge)
- Spec figures on the aggregator sites point to "tuned" fantasy physics in several mods: Gran Torino tuned at 704 bhp, Monte Carlo at 650 hp, Vega at 430 bhp and 335 km/h. — [AssettoWorld Gran Torino tuned](https://www.assettoworld.com/car/ford-gran-torino-1972-351-tuned); [AssettoWorld Monte Carlo](https://www.assettoworld.com/car/chevrolet-monte-carlo-ii-custom-1972)

### Inferences
- For an open-source game, treat SBH/Uncle M/GeroDa74 models as legally unclear. GeroDa74 is the highest risk, because of the Forza/NFS rip allegations and because it is payware. They are fine for local play. They are not safe to ship.
- The game re-derives engine and running gear from its own part tree, so poor mod physics matters less for Street Corsa than model provenance does.

### Gaps
- No source names the original game for each SBH or Uncle M model (for example, whether the 1979 Camaro or the 1972 Gran Torino comes from Forza, GTA, NFS or rFactor). Each one would need checking by hand.
- Encryption status is unverified for every entry. No download was made, as instructed.

## Which allow redistribution or are open/permissive?

### Takeaway
None of the mods found states a licence that allows redistribution. Most pages state no terms at all. The Assettoland, ModsFire and aggregator mirrors are re-uploads, not permission. Nothing in this group can be bundled with an open-source release without asking the authors, and for rips of commercial games permission is not the author's to give.

### Cited Findings
- The 1970 AMC Javelins page has no licence. The author only invites skin contributions ("If anyone would like to make those skins I would happily include them in the pack"). — [Overtake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/1970-amc-javelins.46012/)
- The Brickyard Legends Camaro has no licence statement and depends on many earlier contributors. — [Overtake](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/chevrolet-camaro-ii-imsa-transam-etcc-by-brickyard-legends-team.21998/)
- The TransAm Legends release notes say the re-release was done with SBH's permission ("asked my permission for release") but give no redistribution terms. — [PDF](https://thracing.de/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/2020-03-26-AC-TransAm-Legends-v2.0-Release-Notes-1.pdf)
- GeroDa74 mods are payware (Facebook, then Patreon), and community members still share them on MediaFire. — [Overtake thread](https://www.overtake.gg/threads/how-to-get-geroda74s-mods.204958/)

### Inferences
- The practical route for Street Corsa is to keep these as user-installed AC cars that the game detects, as it already does, and not to ship them. A permissive 1970s US car would probably have to be commissioned or modelled by the project.
- The Javelin author (Joshua Widder, on Overtake) is the most likely person to agree to permission, since the work is his own. Asking him would be worth a try.

### Gaps
- No licence text was found for the Uncle M, SBH, PedroBLR, ACTK, Lucas Macedo or Henrique_Drifter releases. Their Overtake/Patreon pages may hold terms that I did not fetch.
