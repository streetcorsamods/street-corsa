# New 1950-79 AC1 car mods (2025-2026) and the licensing landscape

Researched 2026-10-03. Cars already installed (see `_installed_cars.txt`) are left out. Nothing was downloaded.
Method: Overtake.gg AC Cars category pages 1-2 (sorted by latest update), Overtake tag pages (1970, muscle-car, classic, legion), web searches. Overtake's category has 51 pages sorted by update date, and only 2 were read, so this list is a sample, not a full census.

## A) New or updated 1950-1979 car mods, 2025-01 to 2026-10, and active modders

### Takeaway
Few new **American** 1950-79 road cars came out on Overtake in 2025-2026. The standout find is MJC Solutions' free Australian Chrysler Valiant Charger trilogy (VH/VJ/VK, 1972-76), which is mechanically a Mopar. The rest of the era's new work is European or racing (physics remakes, historic racers, skin packs). The modders the brief named (Reboot Team, Legion, MRN, pb, lovetrain, RPM, exmods, ATCC, Mby, Redd, j8) showed no confirmed new 1950-79 American car releases in the sources I could reach.

## B) Licensing, redistribution, rips, encryption, and what an open-source game can do

### Takeaway
Almost no AC car mods carry a formal open licence (I found no CC-BY/CC0 car mods). The default is "all rights reserved, download from the author", and many modders explicitly object to re-hosting. Overtake's terms forbid deep-linking to its files without permission. Ripped content is common on aggregator and paid sites. Encryption is widespread and exists to keep control, not because of copyright. Comparable tools (AssettoServer / Content Manager) **link to the author's download pages and do not re-host**. Street Corsa should not ship third-party cars. It should ship only content it owns or has written permission for, plus a manifest of cars with source-page links.

### Cited Findings
- Overtake terms: uploaders grant Overtake "a non-exclusive, irrevocable, perpetual, worldwide license to use, distribute, reproduce, and display the Content in connection with the Service". That grant covers Overtake only, not third parties. — [Overtake terms](https://www.overtake.gg/help/terms/)
- Overtake terms prohibit "Deep-linking to our resources and modding files for any purpose without explicit authorization from OverTake". So a game should link to the **resource page**, not the direct file URL. — [Overtake terms](https://www.overtake.gg/help/terms/)
- Overtake terms (as summarised by the fetch tool; check the exact wording) say users must have permission before uploading mods, and prohibit posting "original game files, encrypted files, and other copyrighted materials". — [Overtake terms](https://www.overtake.gg/help/terms/)
- GTPlanet Downloads works the same way: the creator grants the site a non-exclusive licence to publish and keeps ownership and copyright. — [GTPlanet](https://www.gtplanet.net/forum/threads/gtplanet-downloads-for-assetto-corsa-mods-%E2%80%94-now-available.420037/)
- Some creators say outright not to re-host their work. AssettoLand-style re-hosting sites earn ad money from other people's work and seldom update the copies, which leaves users with broken mods after CSP changes. This is the community's main grievance against redistribution. — [GTPlanet thread (search summary)](https://www.gtplanet.net/forum/threads/gtplanet-downloads-for-assetto-corsa-mods-%E2%80%94-now-available.420037/)
- AssettoServer docs (the best comparable open-source project): "Please use the download links the authors of the content you're using provide unless you're explicitly allowed to reupload them". Content Manager's `cm_content/content.json` lets a server point players at a download URL for missing cars and tracks. — [AssettoServer FAQ](https://github.com/compujuckel/assettoserver-docs/blob/master/docs/faq.md)
- Kunos' stance: it does not ban modding, but it banned **ripped content** from copyrighted software on its official forum. The community line between legal and illegal: reusing someone else's models, physics or textures, modified or not, without permission is illegal. — [Overtake thread "Illegal mods - how would I know?"](https://www.overtake.gg/threads/illegal-mods-how-would-i-know.107033/page-2), [Overtake "central modding forum"](https://www.overtake.gg/threads/a-new-central-assetto-corsa-modding-discussion-forum.122766/)
- Rips: community threads say a large share of paid AC mods are rips from other games (Forza and others) with poor physics. One sign is a mod from an unknown author with no work-in-progress history. Theft also goes the other way (AC models taken into GTA V mods). I found no specific public Turn 10 or Rockstar DMCA case against AC mods. — [Overtake "Assetto Drive car mods, legit?"](https://www.overtake.gg/threads/assetto-drive-car-mods-legit.143048/), [GTPlanet mods discussion p.271](https://www.gtplanet.net/forum/threads/assetto-corsa-pc-mods-general-discussion.307899/page-271)
- Encryption: community discussion says KN5 encryption "has nothing to do with copyright". Modders use it to keep control and stop their work being copied or edited. There are two kinds: "heavy" (model and data both locked) and "soft" (model encrypted, data editable; RSS, VRC and similar use this). Critics say it stops users from fixing errors. Decryption tools are passed around (ResHax). — [GTPlanet mods discussion](https://www.gtplanet.net/forum/threads/assetto-corsa-pc-mods-general-discussion.307899/page-2151), [ResHax](https://reshax.com/topic/829-decryptingunpacking-encrypted-assetto-corsa-kn5-models/)
- Re-host aggregators (acsupply.cx, assettocorsamods.io/.org, assettoworld, stmods, modland) re-host freely, provenance is often unclear, and some sell content. Avoid them as sources. — [acsupply](https://www.acsupply.cx/browse/cars), [assettocorsamods.org](https://www.assettocorsamods.org/product/assetto-corsa-classic-revolution-mod/)

### Inferences
- **Shipping cars:** the repo should not bundle third-party AC car folders. Without an explicit licence, the work is all rights reserved, and most car mods also use real-brand trademarks. Ship only (a) cars Street Corsa's own team made, or (b) cars whose authors gave written permission, recorded in a CREDITS/PERMISSIONS file.
- **Recommended pattern:** keep a `cars.json` entry per supported car with the folder id, the author, the **resource page** URL (Overtake or the author's Patreon or site, never a deep file link or an aggregator), version, and a "free/paid/encrypted" flag. On first run, scan `content/cars`, show what is missing with "Get it" links, and work with whatever is installed. This copies the AssettoServer / Content Manager "missing content" approach.
- Excluding encrypted cars is consistent with this. Encrypted cars are also the ones the game cannot inspect or modify (parts/sound swaps via CarDataOverlay).
- Be wary of anything from aggregators, or from unknown authors with no WIP history (likely rips). Treat them as non-shippable and possibly non-linkable.
- Easy win: ask friendly free modders such as MJC Solutions ("mods will always be free") for permission to redistribute, or to sign off on a pinned version.

### Gaps
- I found no AC car mod under an explicit CC-BY/CC0 licence. If any exist, they are rare and need a targeted search (GitHub, Sketchfab-based converts).
- I did not verify Overtake's exact clause on "encrypted files". The wording came through a summarising fetch. Confirm it on the live terms page.
- I found no documented DMCA action by Kunos, Turn 10 or Rockstar against specific AC car mods in 2025-26.
- I did not survey per-modder readme licence clauses for the installed authors (pb, lovetrain, RPM, exmods, ATCC, MRN). Reading each installed car's readme/ui_car.json `author`/`url` locally would be a cheap next step.
