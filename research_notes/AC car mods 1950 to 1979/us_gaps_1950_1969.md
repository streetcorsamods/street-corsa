# AC1 car mods of 1950-1969 American cars missing from the Street Corsa install

Scope: Assetto Corsa 1 only, model years 1950-1969, US makes. Excludes everything in `_installed_cars.txt`. Researched 2026-10-03, nothing downloaded.

Overall caveat: most hits come from **reupload/aggregator hosts** (assettoworld.com, assettocorsamods.io, assettoland10.wixsite.com via Modsfire, modslab, modland). They rarely name the author, the model source or a licence, and several returned HTTP 403 when fetched, so details could not be checked. Only Overtake.gg pages and the AC Legends release notes gave first-hand author or credit data.

## Q1. Which findable mods fill the gaps? (catalogue, one entry per car)

### Takeaway
There are real gap-fillers, mostly in the Chrysler, Pontiac, Hudson, Mercury, Thunderbird and Chevrolet-variant groups. Most come from the Smallblockhero / AC Legends / Uncle M family or from Lovetrain, and they are spread through reupload hosts. For many of the makes on the list I found **no AC1 mod at all**: Studebaker Avanti/Hawk/Lark, Oldsmobile Toronado/Rocket 88, Buick Skylark/Wildcat, Chrysler 300 letter cars, DeSoto, Edsel, Nash, Kaiser, Willys, Packard (1950+), AMC AMX/Rambler, 1960s F-100/Apache/Dodge/International trucks, Falcon US, Ranchero and Torino 68-69.

### Inferences
- The biggest real gaps the community **has** filled are: 1967 Plymouth GTX, 1968 Dart Hemi Super Stock, 1968 Bonneville, 1969 Trans Am, the 1968 Camaro, the 1961 Impala, the 1967 Cougar, the 1958 Thunderbird, the 1966 GT350 R and the Stereo 1950s trio (56 Bel Air, 57 Mercury Commuter, 59 Dodge Coronet).
- Almost all the Chrysler and Pontiac 1960s mods trace back to the Smallblockhero / AC Legends / Uncle M family. So the same provenance problem (below) applies to most of the catalogue.
- Stereo's 1950s Big 3 is the most "clean" find: an original model, free, on Overtake. Its physics are AI-traffic grade, though, and it is not built for racing.

### Gaps
- Exact versions, update dates, CSP features and encryption for most aggregator entries: AssettoWorld and assettocorsamods.io returned 403, and Assettoland lists only the author and version.
- Paid Patreon/Ko-fi/Gumroad offerings: Patreon pages could not be read (403), so prices are unknown.
- I did not search assettocorsa.club or the r/assettocorsa and YouTube descriptions in depth (I ran out of the research budget). These may hold more 1950s cars and trucks.

## Q2. Quality and model origin (original vs rip)

### Takeaway
The Smallblockhero / AC Legends family openly credits Forza and other games as model sources, so most 1960s muscle and pony car mods are **conversions (rips)**. Original models are rare: Stereo's traffic trio, and possibly Lovetrain's cars (not verified).

### Cited Findings
- Smallblockhero / Assetto Legends: "3D models are credited to Turn10 Forza, SimBin, Retro Expansion, Grid, AAR Cuda, and Smallblockhero". — [DrivingItalia AC Legends thread (via search)](https://www.drivingitalia.net/forums/topic/78229-ac-legends-mod-gt-legends-in-assetto-corsa/)
- TransAm Legends v2.0 credits "Conversions/3D Modelling: SmallBlock Hero, mickstix, Uncle M". Its sounds come from "Kunos, various unknown sources", and "due to various sources the model quality may vary per car". v2.0 overhauled the physics (Bazza) and added damage models and 3 LODs per car. — [Release notes PDF](https://thracing.de/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/2020-03-26-AC-TransAm-Legends-v2.0-Release-Notes-1.pdf)
- Stereo's Big 3 is an original creation with "limited data" physics, made for AI traffic. — [Overtake.gg](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/1950s-american-traffic-big-3.51906/)
- Two 1969 Charger mods (Ilya Dobrovolsky; Nabaoui & SMPS) are encrypted. — [Assettoland Dodge](https://assettoland10.wixsite.com/assettoland/dodge)
- Forum threads discuss how to tell "illegal mods" (rips) apart from legitimate ones, and Smallblockhero is a recurring topic there. — [Overtake thread](https://www.overtake.gg/threads/illegal-mods-how-would-i-know.107033/page-3)

### Inferences
- For an open-source game, any car built on Smallblockhero, AC Legends, Uncle M or Donnie Heerdt work should be treated as a **Forza/other-game rip**. Do not ship or bundle it, at most let the player point the game at their own install. That covers most of the catalogue above.

### Gaps
- Model origin of the Lovetrain cars (Hudson, Impalas), the Velo & Tim Leighton 1961 Impala, the DV Gamemods cars, Pods Chevelle, the RBRC/Patreon Thunderbirds and the GT350 R is not known.

## Q3. Licence / redistribution terms

### Takeaway
None of the sources I reached gives an explicit licence that allows redistribution. Treat every entry as "may install for personal use, may not be bundled" unless the author gives written permission.

### Cited Findings
- GrippedUp's pack is a "community preservation pack": "original credit remains with their original creators", and it invites creators to complain. In other words it redistributes **without permission**. — [GrippedUp](https://grippedupmods.com/downloads/car-packs/wild-stallions-muscle-car-pack)
- Stereo's Big 3 and AtoomDemoon's Hornet skins state no licence, only a donation link. — [Overtake Big 3](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/1950s-american-traffic-big-3.51906/); [Overtake Hornet skins](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/fabulous-hudson-hornet.61807/)
- The TransAm Legends v2.0 notes contain no licence section. They do record that the release was done "with permission" between Team SBH and mickstix. — [Release notes PDF](https://thracing.de/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/2020-03-26-AC-TransAm-Legends-v2.0-Release-Notes-1.pdf)

### Inferences
- The best candidates to ask for permission are authors of **original** models who publish on Overtake: Stereo for the 1950s trio, and possibly Lovetrain Cody if his models turn out to be original.

### Gaps
- Licence terms for Patreon/Ko-fi releases (BCRC, the 1958 Thunderbird author, SimCarCollector) could not be read.
