station.kn5 is made in part from these models, each licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):

- "Vintage Gas Station" by Wardster (https://sketchfab.com/WardsterSAW), the office:
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/vintage-gas-station-107d8dd7948040e0b8fee2bede0fc2b4
  Changes: its decals of neglect (moss, leaks, damage) left out.
- "Vintage Gas Pump" by Rescue3D Assets (https://sketchfab.com/rescue3d), the four pumps:
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/vintage-gas-pump-0096f206ce1a4381bea58f9f94fc6b1c
- "Gasstation Sign - 2048px²" by Mark Peters, the pole sign by the road:
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/gasstation-sign-2048px2-11416edc56e941b7b10bcc87a6dc0ee7
- "Old Tire Inflator" by gorzi, the air meter at the office's corner:
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/old-tire-inflator-3ab1bef1829b44a0857f0287a670c5f0
  Changes: one of its two meters kept, scaled to 1.26 m, brought down to 4000 triangles.
- "Vintage Soda Machien" by Han1993, the soda machine by the door:
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/vintage-soda-machien-05b7b54f12a74e659a8561614e63aee2
  Changes: scaled from centimetres to metres.

All of them set into the scene, their materials turned into AC's ksPerPixelMultiMap (pictures scaled to 1536 at most,
the PBR maps turned into txMaps) and converted to KN5 with the rest of the station (tools/StationScene:
compose_station.py, prepare_prop.py, StationSceneBuilder; build_station.py runs them).

The rest of the station: the forecourt, the road, the islands and the canopy drawn by compose_station.py; the bench,
trash can, chalkboard, shelves, oil tins, tyres, rim, barrels, jerrycan, crates, hand truck, covered car, utility box,
picnic table, street lamps, fence, bushes and poles are Poly Haven models and the ground's surfaces Poly Haven textures
(CC0, https://polyhaven.com/license); the skies are Poly Haven panoramas (CC0).

The attendant is a prop of his own (props/attendant): "Speaking Man with Talking Gestures" by LasquetiSpice, CC BY 4.0,
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/speaking-man-with-talking-gestures-c55bcf894e1b4da1adf0cf258fe94c9f. Changes: his
sweater painted as a work shirt, four unweighted bones (the top of the head, three finger tips) folded into their
parents so the rig fits AC's 64, his skin and his talk converted to KN5 and ksanim (tools/Props: prepare_actor.py,
ActorBuilder).
