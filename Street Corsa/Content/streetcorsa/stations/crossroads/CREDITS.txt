The cars are scaled to their length, their paint made less metallic and their pictures brought down to 1024.
All of them set into the scene, their materials turned into AC's ksPerPixelMultiMap (pictures scaled to 1536 at most,
the PBR maps turned into txMaps) and converted to KN5 with the rest of the station (tools/StationScene:
compose_station.py, prepare_prop.py, StationSceneBuilder; build_station.py runs them).

The rest of the station: the forecourt, the road, the islands, the canopy, the land, the wires and the trees' shades
drawn by compose_station.py; the bench, trash can, chalkboard, shelves, oil tins, tyres, rim, barrels, jerrycan,
crates, hand truck, covered car, utility box, picnic table, street lamps, fence, bushes, weeds, boulders, fallen trunk,
stumps and poles are Poly Haven models, the trees, bushes and grass clumps on cards are pictures of Poly Haven models
(island_tree_02, searsia_lucida, shrub_02, grass_medium_02, rendered by bake_cards.py), and the ground's surfaces are
Poly Haven textures (CC0, https://polyhaven.com/license); the skies are Poly Haven panoramas (CC0).

The attendant is a prop of his own (props/attendant): "Speaking Man with Talking Gestures" by LasquetiSpice, CC BY 4.0,
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/speaking-man-with-talking-gestures-c55bcf894e1b4da1adf0cf258fe94c9f. Changes: his
sweater painted as a work shirt, four unweighted bones (the top of the head, three finger tips) folded into their
parents so the rig fits AC's 64, his skin and his talk converted to KN5 and ksanim (tools/Props: prepare_actor.py,
ActorBuilder).
