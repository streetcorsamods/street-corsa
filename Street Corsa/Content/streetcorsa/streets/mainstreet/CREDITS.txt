The cars are scaled to their length, brought down to 8000 triangles each (5000 those further from the player's
curb), their paint made less metallic. Every model
is converted to AC's ksPerPixelMultiMap (its pictures scaled down, the PBR maps turned into txMaps) and to KN5 with the
rest of the street (tools/DinerScene: compose_street.py, prepare_prop.py, DinerSceneBuilder).

The diners' looks (the Hi-Way Grill, Surfside and the Starlite are this corner dressed three more ways, each shown for
its diner alone): the models above named for them, Zhabotinsky's and robinmikart's cars again in the lots, and our own
roof signs, billboard ads, drums, fence, pennants, surfboards and neon.

The rest of the street: the Downtown City MegaKit by Quaternius (CC0, https://quaternius.com) for the roads, sidewalks
and the lot; our own houses, signs, billboard ads and wires; Poly Haven's textures and skies, and its jacaranda_tree,
island_tree_02, tree_small_02, searsia_lucida, flower_gazania and periwinkle_plant as pictures on crossed cards for the
lawns' trees, bushes and flowers (CC0, https://polyhaven.com/license).
