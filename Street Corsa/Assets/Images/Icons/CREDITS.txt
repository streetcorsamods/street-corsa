The game's icons: where each comes from, who made it and under what terms. The owner retouched them into 512 px
black glyphs on transparent, each under the name the game's theme knows it by (icons/<name>).

Every icon's author, source, licence and page are in credits.csv, next to this file: the one list, installed with
the game in Assets\Images\Icons, and the one the game's Settings build their icon credits from (in code, not in a text
pool a translation or a mod could change). Add a row there for an icon that comes in.

The terms:
- freeicons.io's free icons are under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/): the icon credited to its author and the site.
- The Noun Project's free icons are CC BY 3.0 as well: "<name> by <author> from Noun Project".
- Vecteezy's Free License (https://www.vecteezy.com/licensing-agreement) asks for the credit "Vecteezy.com" beside
  the author.
- magnific.com's free licence asks for its attribution: "Icon by dDara", linked to the icon's page.
- Pixabay's Content License (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) asks for no credit; it is given anyway.
  freesvg.org's are public domain.
- "Street Corsa": drawn for the game by its owner, Pablo Camardelli (car_parts, engine_stand and high_beam, the owner's
  word, 2026-10-31), under CONTENT-LICENSE.txt (CC BY 4.0). car_lifted is the owner's change of freeicons.io's "car"
  and stays under that icon's terms.

Not open licences: car_key (Vecteezy), disc_brake (magnific.com) and driving_wheel (Pixabay) are used under their
sites' licences, which allow them inside a work such as this game but not taken out and shared or sold on their own.
They are in this repository only as part of Street Corsa; they are not under CONTENT-LICENSE.txt or the MIT licence.
Reuse them apart from the game, or in a fork, only under a licence of your own from their site, or put icons of your
own in their place.
