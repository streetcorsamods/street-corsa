The game's icons: where each comes from, who made it and under what terms. The owner retouched them into 512 px
black glyphs on transparent, each under the name the game's theme knows it by (icons/<name>).

Every icon's author, source, licence and page are in credits.csv, next to this file: the one list, installed with
the game in Assets\Images\Icons, and the one the game's Settings build their icon credits from (in code, not in a text
pool a translation or a mod could change). Add a row there for an icon that comes in, with the licence its own page
shows: on freeicons.io each icon has its own, under "Free icon" (not every free icon there is CC BY).

The terms:
- Most are freeicons.io's or the Noun Project's under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/): each credited to its author and the site.
- freesvg.org's are public domain.
- "Street Corsa": drawn for the game by its owner, Pablo Camardelli (car_parts, engine_stand and high_beam, the owner's
  word, 2026-10-31), under CONTENT-LICENSE.txt (CC BY 4.0). car_lifted is the owner's change of freeicons.io's "car"
  (the car on a lift post) and stays under that icon's terms.

Not open licences, to be replaced (2026-11-01), each under its own terms meanwhile:
- wheel (ColourCreatype, freeicons.io): free for personal use only.
- garage, parts_filled and quit (ColourCreatype, freeicons.io): CC BY-NC 3.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/), non-commercial use only.
- handshake (Aficons, freeicons.io): free for commercial use with a link to the author; not an open licence.
- driving_wheel (OpenClipart-Vectors, Pixabay): the Pixabay Content License
  (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/), which allows it inside a work such as this game but not taken out
  and shared or sold on its own.
- double_gears (Md. Nuruddin, freeicons.io): its page and exact licence were not found.
These are in this repository only as part of Street Corsa; they are not under CONTENT-LICENSE.txt or the MIT licence.
Reuse them apart from the game, or in a fork, only under their own terms, or put icons of your own in their place.

On 2026-10-20 these were swapped for Tabler Icons and back the same day: the owner prefers these. On 2026-10-31
