# First steps

## New game

1. **NEW GAME**, type your name.
2. Pick a difficulty: **Easy**, **Normal** or **Hard**.
3. **Start Game**.

The game saves as you go.

## Your first day

You start with money and no car.

1. **Car dealers** (garage, bottom left): drive to a lot, pick a car, **BUY IT**.
   Or look in the **Newspaper** for private sellers.
2. **Gas station**: fill the tank. Fuel and oil carry over between races.
3. **Go race!**: pick a diner and challenge someone, or enter an invitation.
4. Back in the garage, spend your winnings on parts.

## Time

- Days run from 8:00 to 22:00. Everything takes time: driving, shopping, racing, wrenching.
- **Calendar → End Day** skips to tomorrow morning.
- Overnight, shops restock, rivals race each other and new races are posted.

## Winning

Open **Career** (top right) and press **Pursue** on a victory path, for example:

- **Street Legend**: reach 100 reputation
- **Dethrone the King**: beat the King for his pink slip

You can keep racing after you win.
