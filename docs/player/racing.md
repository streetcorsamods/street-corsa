# Racing

Press **Go race!** in the garage.

## Where

- **Diners**: pick a racer, a race and a bet. Each diner has a horsepower class; the fast ones won't race a slow car.
- **The street** (**CRUISE THE STREETS** at a diner): wait at the curb, someone pulls up and offers a race.
- **Invitations**: events with prizes and entry rules.
- **Free Run** and **Test & Tune**: practice, nothing at stake.

## Races

- **Drag**: a quarter mile. Can be a **bracket race**: run quicker than your dial-in and you lose.
- **Road race**: a lap of a circuit.
- **Time attack**: three laps, best lap wins.

## Bets

- **Cash**: bigger at night and with well-known racers.
- **Pink slips**: the winner takes the loser's car.

Jumping the start is no contest. Quitting a race counts as a loss.

## Police

Road races on the street can draw the cops, more often at night. Cross the finish line to be safe.

- **Busted**: you lose the race, pay a fine and your car goes to the impound. **Collect** it from the garage.
- **Escaped**: a little reputation.

## After the race

Damage, fuel and oil carry over. A wreck is towed home. Check **Repairs** before the next one.
