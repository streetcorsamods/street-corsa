# The garage

## Moving around

- **Drag** to walk round the car, **scroll** to get closer.
- **Key** starts the engine. Hold **W** or **Space** to rev.

## Working on parts

1. Press **Parts**. The car turns see-through.
2. Click a part, then **Take it off**. It goes to your **PARTS SHELF**.
3. To fit a part, pick it on the shelf: the places it fits glow green. Click one.

Parts come off in order, like a real car: the game tells you what's in the way.

- **Lift**: raise the car to get at wheels, brakes and the rear axle.
- **Pull the engine**: it goes onto the **Stand**, where it comes apart. **Turn over** flips it.
- **Engine kits** arrive in a crate: assemble on the stand or straight into the car.

## Keeping it alive

- **WORK ON IT** (on a part's card): rebuild it with a kit, or swap it at the counter.
- **Repairs**: race damage, body work, rust.
- The engine sheet shows **Power** and **Torque**. Every part changes them.

Worn parts make the car slower. Rust grows every day.

## Engines

The base game has no engine packs: a car runs on its own Assetto Corsa engine until you install one as a mod. With an
engine pack, any block fits any car with an empty engine bay.
