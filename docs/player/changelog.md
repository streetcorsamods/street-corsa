# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- **Uninstall asks about your things.** Tick **Keep my saves, settings and mods** to keep them for the next install,
  or leave it unticked to remove them with the game. An uninstall without the wizard (winget, `msiexec /x`) always
  keeps them.
- **The uninstaller says what it did**, including when Assetto Corsa was still running and kept a race's changes.
- **Mods installed with the Mod Manager play in your next game** without restarting Street Corsa.
- **New mods show up in an old save at once.** Loading a game with new or updated mods restocks the part shops and
  junkyards and refreshes the car lots and the paper. Your cars and the parts you sold to a junkyard stay. A note
  tells you what changed.
- A loader covers the screen while mods install.
- The installer and Windows' Apps list show the game's own icon.
