# Changelog

## 0.1.8

- **Engine sounds without the hiccup.** Held at steady revs, every engine used to drop out for a tenth of a second
  about every five seconds, the point where its recording looped. Each loop now joins where its end is in step with
  its start, so the sound runs on smoothly, and the cockpit sound no longer clicks at the same point.
- **For modders:** all 38 Street Corsa engine sounds in the 17 engine packs are rebuilt this way, with the same
  events, levels and GUIDs; the packs' trunks on SourceForge have them, and each pack's next release brings them.

## 0.1.7

- **Street Rod 0.5.1: the machine shop, kitted out.** Gardena Machine & Engine is a real engine shop now: a column
  boring bar and a long crank grinder with its wheel in the guard where the jobs go on, a hydraulic press, lathes, a
  head surfacer, bench and floor drill presses, an engine crane with a 454 hanging from it, a big-block on a stand and
  an overhead crane.
- Round them, the clutter of a working shop: a belt-drive compressor, welding bottles on their hand truck, a red
  rollaway, a vise and pistons on the workbench, a valve grinder on the head bench, oil cans and drums, caged lamps
  over the machines and a 1950s clock on the wall.
- **For modders:** the machines and props are downloads from Sketchfab (CC BY 4.0) and Poly Haven (CC0), their makers
  named in the scene's credits; Street Rod shops 1.1.0 is on SourceForge.

## 0.1.6

- **Street Rod 0.5.0: a town round Main Street.** About five blocks by five: downtown's shops, the houses, the strip
  up north, the beach road, the highway out east and a farm at the edge, with mountains on the sky to the north.
- **The four diners each have a place of their own.** The Main Street Diner stays downtown; the Hi-Way Grill is a
  truck stop out on the highway, Surfside Coffee Shop faces the sea on the beach road, and the Starlite sits on the
  neon strip. Each has its own lot, its own way in and its own parking bays.
- **Private sellers show their cars all over town.** A car from the paper's private ads stands on a house's drive, at
  a curb, on a vacant lot with a FOR SALE sign, out back of the shops or in a farm yard, the same place every time
  you go and see it. The screen tells you where.
- **The gas station looks lived in.** Parked cars that change from day to day, the edge of town round it, a back
  yard, a farm up the road, and the attendant in a proper 50s uniform and cap.
- The houses' gardens have real hedges and shrubs, and their plants stay off the porches and the paths to the doors.
- The town is drawn near your car and seen far off past it, so it stays smooth.
- **For modders:** Street Rod's street kit (the shops, houses, parked cars, trees and street furniture the places are
  laid out with) is a mod of its own on SourceForge, shared by Main Street and the gas station. A street can now be a
  town with every diner at its own place, and can name its private sellers' spots. See the modding guide.

## 0.1.5

- **No more busts by a cop you never saw.** A speed-trap cop that Assetto Corsa moved to the pits goes back to its
  trap, and only a cop that really gets past you pulls you over.
- **The garage camera stands in front of the engine hoist**, not behind it. Zoom with the wheel to get past it.
- **A car in the impound isn't shown in the garage.** The note and the Collect button show where it is.
- **Smoother moving scenes.** A car driving in, the car lot, the cruise street and the main screen's fades cost less
  to draw, and lamps keep their shadows while a car moves.
- A tow is charged only for the car that was towed: an earlier race's crash or bust no longer bills the car you race
  now.
- A cracked windscreen from a crash stays cracked. The garage lamps come back after the 3D view restarts. The car
  lot's camera goes back to the car after a scene.
- Race results are saved safely when another program holds the file for a moment.
- Fixes to engine figures (an exhaust that flows nothing, engine speeds past what a part allows) and the Tune
  screen's buttons. The matchup's numbers follow your language.
- New help page: **People who talk back**, the AI chat with sellers, salesmen and rivals: setup, providers and what
  gets sent.
- **For modders:** a setting can now have scored runs alone on public roads in two-way traffic made from your own
  cars, Challenges with medals, car tiers opened by score, a showroom and high-score tables, a Home front page, a
  road picker, a 3D Challenges screen, and a garage laid out as a showroom to pick a car in. See the modding guide.

## 0.1.4

- **Street Rod 0.4.0: its places are mods of their own.** The garage, the gas station, the shops (the paint booth, the
  part and machine shops, the warehouse) and Main Street with its diners each come in a download of their own, with
  their own version, so they can be swapped. They come inside Street Rod's download: installing it from
  **Mod Manager → Install mods** puts in the setting and all four. Your saves carry over.
- **Modders can replace a place.** A mod that plays in Street Rod can put in a garage, a station, a room or street
  pieces of its own, or take Street Rod's out. Each place's files are on SourceForge; see the modding guide.
- Each place brings its own sounds: the garage's air goes with the garage, the street's and the diners' with Main
  Street.

## 0.1.3

- **Every engine the right way round.** The engine packs were built as mirror images of the real engines; all twelve
  are fixed. Download the new versions and install them from **Mod Manager → Install mods**.
- **Street Rod 0.1.1.** The carburettors, air cleaners and blower that fit any engine are the right way round too.
  Every rim and tyre has a model again, two wheels that faced the wrong way are fixed, and a few cars' engines sit
  better in their bays.
- **Your car comes home after a race.** Whenever it can still run, it drives into the garage. A car you win comes
  home and is the one selected. A car that won't run is towed home, and you pay the tow.
- **Lamps cast shadows indoors.** The garage, the shops, the car lot and the paint booth are lit by their lamps, so
  cars and props stand on the floor, day and night.
- **Smoother moving scenes.** A car driving in, the lift, the engine hoist and a revving engine cost less to draw.
- **The garage's wheels and springs.** A tyre you take off is gone from the car. The car comes down off the lift only
  with a tyre on every rim. A bare rim shows and can be picked. A rear corner takes one spring, coil or leaf.
- Lowered cars keep their tyres' shadows on the floor.
- Many fixes from a code audit: safer mod installs, each rival in a field race gets their own result, and a quicker
  first start after a race.

## 0.1.2

- **A new racer window at the diner.** Back and close sit in the window's title bar. The race, the matchup and the
  stakes are side by side, and cash or pink slips is one click.
- **Pick the diner's track from cards** with pictures, a search box and a DRAG or ROAD tag on each.
- **A rival's reputation tells you more.** Hover the figure beside their name to see what their standing means and how
  they stack up against you.
- **The race result card in Assetto Corsa says what was at stake.** A pink-slip win or loss now says so, and the card
  is bigger and easier to read.
- **New Assetto Corsa cars show up in your next game** without restarting Street Corsa. A new Assetto Corsa folder in
  Settings takes effect at once.
- A step line and a progress bar show what the game is doing while it loads.
- **Settings** sit in two columns on a wide screen, with one Save button for what you type. The credits have a screen
  of their own.
- The Mod Manager's buttons are along the top.

## 0.1.1

- **Uninstall asks about your things.** Tick **Keep my saves, settings and mods** to keep them for the next install,
  or leave it unticked to remove them with the game. An uninstall without the wizard (winget, `msiexec /x`) always
  keeps them.
- **The uninstaller says what it did**, including when Assetto Corsa was still running and kept a race's changes.
- **Mods installed with the Mod Manager play in your next game** without restarting Street Corsa.
- **New mods show up in an old save at once.** Loading a game with new or updated mods restocks the part shops and
  junkyards and refreshes the car lots and the paper. Your cars and the parts you sold to a junkyard stay. A note
  tells you what changed.
- A loader covers the screen while mods install.
- The installer and Windows' Apps list show the game's own icon.
