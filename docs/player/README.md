# Player guide

Street Corsa is a car career on top of Assetto Corsa. You buy cars, build them in your garage and race them. The
races run in Assetto Corsa.

The game itself is the core: the garage and racing. A **setting** mod makes it a career (its town, its cars, its
places). Pick one at **New Game**.
