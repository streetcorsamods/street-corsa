## Executive summary

22 bugs (15 *Plausible*), 24 stability, 10 security/integrity, 27 duplication clusters (9 drifting), 14 pattern deviations, 13 performance, 8 clean-code. That is a smaller count per line of code than the last audit, with 60k new lines.

The integrity core holds: the AC restore paths now share `KeptFile` and every restore failure reaches the player. The launcher refuses to start under a running AC. The scene loaders confine their models, and model replies never decide a price. The XAML has no hex colours left (it had 18), and every new `async void` is wrapped.

What is new is a **network surface**: the opt-in AI. Most of the security findings are mod paths that missed `TryCombineUnder`, plus the AI key.

Top fixes now:

1. **Two money loops in the economy, plus the paper's offers.** Private-ad cars (0.68–1.02 × worth) resell through the paper at worth or more, risk-free, in about a week. Used parts in the paper on Easy cost less than the junkyard pays for them. A paper buyer's offer also stands at full price after the player strips the car. The first loop is a balance call for you, since flipping may be wanted. The other two are bugs.
2. **The AI key goes wherever the settings point.** One saved key is sent to any provider and address, including Ollama, whose key box is hidden, and plain `http://`. A mod's ComfyUI graph also runs whatever custom nodes the player has installed.
3. **The garage leaks its whole view, 3D viewport included, on every visit** (`GarageScreenView.xaml.cs:48`, E8). Right behind it:
   - Quitting during a radio library scan can free FMOD under a worker.
   - In AC, the hood raycast passes culling `0` ("backfaces only"), so parts under a hood may be drawn through it.
   - `ObjModel` builds the next prop inside-out (a regression from e338037).

## BUGS (must fix)

### Economy / gameplay

- ✔ **Services/Market/CarSaleService.cs:174-225**: a paper buyer's offer is fixed in `ReviewAds` from that day's worth, and `AcceptOfferAsync` never values the car again. The car stays workable for the 2 days the offer stands.
  - **Why:** take out the engine or the rims (about 40% of base price per `missingEngineShare`), accept, and get the full offer while keeping the parts.
  - **Fix:** store `ValueAtOffer` on the offer. At acceptance, if `_market.ValueOf(car)` is lower, withdraw the offer ("the buyer saw it's been stripped") or scale `Amount` by now/then.
- ✔ **Services/Market/UsedCarMarketService.cs:575-582 ↔ CarSaleService.cs:309-326**: private ads are priced at `privatePriceShare` 0.85 × worth ± 20% (0.68–1.02), before any haggling in the talk. The player's paper ad sells at the asking price when it is at or under worth, and never below worth when over it (`OfferAmount`'s clamp), with up to a 60% daily call chance.
  - **Why:** buy private, list at worth to 1.2 ×, sell: 15–40% a car for a $10 fee, every week, with no risk. **This is a balance call for you:** flipping may be welcome (the CMS vision), but at this margin it is free money.
  - **Fix:** let paper buyers haggle below worth (clamp to `value × dealerShare … asking`), or lower the call chance for a car bought in the last N days.
- **Services/Parts/RivalPartAds.cs:26-27, Services/Parts/NewspaperPartsService.cs:208-209 vs Rules/PartShopRules.cs:78, Services/PartShops/PartShopService.cs:155**: on Easy, a rival's used-part ad asks 0.38–0.46 × worth and a private one 0.41–0.61 ×. The comment on `PartShopRules.BuyFactor` promises this can't happen, but it only checks new parts. The rivals' part ads also reuse the *car* ask shares.
  - **Fix:** apply `PartPriceMultiplier` to `BuyingPrice`/`TradeIn`, or floor every used ask at the highest shop buy price. Give rival part ads their own rules figures. Add a test: no ask below any shop's buy price at every difficulty.
- ✔ *Plausible* **Screens/PaintBooth/PaintBoothScreenViewModel.cs:562-642**: the money is taken and the car repainted before `spent = SpendAsync(...)`, and the `!IsActive` returns in the coat loop skip both `await spent` and `Save()`. - *Plausible* **Screens/Garage/GarageScreenViewModel.cs:840-882 (OnGasStation :544, OnPaintBooth :585, DriveOutTo :623)**: each tile's `AsyncRelayCommand` guards only itself. ### AI / talk

- ✔ *Plausible* **Services/Talk/Haggle.cs:93-99**: the thousands-grouped branch (`\d{1,3}(?:[,.]\d{3})+`, no length cap) uses `decimal.Parse`, then `value *= 1000m`. A typed "1,000,000,…" of 30 or more digits, or a 29-digit number followed by "k", throws `OverflowException`. The line is already in `_playerSaid`, and `KnownSums` re-parses it on every answer, so every later reply in that talk fails ("the line went dead"). A model answer with such a number fails the same way through `Strays`.
  - **Fix:** `TryParse` in the grouped branch, cap `num` at about 12 digits in the regex, and return null rather than overflow on `*1000`.

### Race / AC

- ✔ **apps/new-modes/sc_race/car_parts.lua:353-354**: `root:raycast(ray, nil, nil, nil, nil, 0)` passes culling `0`. The SDK (`lib_scene.lua:674`) documents that as "**0 to hit backfaces only**; -1 to disable backface culling". The comment and the intent (6e47e88) are "both sides of every face". On a one-sided hood the ray misses the top, so `standsOut` judges an air cleaner or carb under the hood as poking out and draws it through the hood. The harness stub ignores the culling argument (`tools/sc_race_harness/test_parts.py:137-140`).
  - **Fix:** pass `-1`, and make the stub honour culling so a test pins it. Whether it shows depends on each car's hood meshes, which may be why play testing didn't catch it.
- ✔ *Plausible* **Services/Race/RaceResultProcessor.cs:139, 867-872**: `ScoreCard` runs after `ApplyAndSave`. `LapTime` → `TimeSpan.FromMilliseconds` throws on NaN, ±∞ or anything over ~9.2e14 ms, and the validator bounds only distance (`RaceResultValidator.cs:246`). The ingestion then quarantines a result that was already applied and saved, and tells the player "nothing was changed". A mod's format module writes `score`.
  - **Fix:** reject a non-finite or out-of-range `score`/`lap_times_ms` in `RaceResultValidator`, and clamp in `LapTime`.
- *Plausible* **Parts/Logic/PartFigures.cs:256-281 → Parts/Export/AcEngineData.cs:136, 171, 219**: `idle_rpm` is only checked for a set value below 0. A mod's `"idle_rpm": 9000` or `{ "add": -900 }` puts `ENGINE_DATA MINIMUM` above `LIMITER` or below 0, and drags `DAMAGE RPM_THRESHOLD` and the downshift point with it.
  - **Fix:** clamp the applied idle to between ~300 and 0.5 × the limiter in `EngineEvaluator`, or report it as `Unrunnable` does.

## SECURITY / INTEGRITY (should fix)

- ✔ **Services/Ai/AiTextService.cs:396-404 + Services/Ai/AiProviders.cs:107-120, 149-152 + Screens/Settings/AiSettingsSection.cs:329-343, 385**: one saved API key is sent to whatever provider and address are set (CWE-522). Save keeps the old `ApiKeyProtected` when no new key is typed, and `OllamaTextProvider` gets the key too (the base class adds `Authorization: Bearer`). Switching from Anthropic or OpenRouter to Ollama (key box hidden), or to another address, sends the paid key there, over plain `http://` if that is the address. Test does the same for a typed but unsaved endpoint. This contradicts `docs/ai-integration.md:23`.
  - **Fix:**
    - Save the provider and endpoint the key belongs to, and send it only while both match.
    - Never pass a key to Ollama unless one was typed for it.
    - Refuse, or warn about, a key over `http://` to a host that isn't loopback.
- **Services/Ai/AiPrompts.cs:392-418 + Services/Ai/ComfyImageService.cs:114-140**: a mod's `ai/comfy_*.json` graph is posted to the player's ComfyUI as written. Its node types are never checked, so any mod can run the player's installed custom nodes (script, exec, file-writing) or save files under any prefix (CWE-94/CWE-73).
  - **Fix:** allow-list the core portrait `class_type`s, leave out other workflows with a problem line, and force the save nodes' `filename_prefix`.
- ✔ **Audio/SoundCues.cs:111**: cue files are resolved with `Path.GetFullPath(Path.Combine(dir, relative))`, unconfined. A mod's `..\..\` or UNC path (`\\host\share\x.ogg`) is probed with `File.Exists` and opened by FMOD at start-up. A UNC path makes Windows authenticate to that host, which leaks the NTLM hash (CWE-22/CWE-200).
  - **Fix:** `PathNames.TryCombineUnder(modFolder, relative, out path)`, as every other content loader does.
