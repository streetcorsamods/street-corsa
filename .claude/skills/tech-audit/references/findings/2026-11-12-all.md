## Executive summary

9 bugs / 15 stability / 2 security / 16 duplication / 15 pattern / 48 performance / 16 clean code. No vulnerable packages, a clean build, and nothing that leaves the AC install changed.

Car animations: the stutter on a moving car (drive-in, drive-off, lift, engine blips) comes from GPU work done every frame, not from the managed code. Top 3:
1. ✔ **Reflection passes defeat the lamp-shadow budget** (`GarageRenderer.Lamps.cs`): AcTools clears and invalidates every lamp's map in the reflection pass (`shadowsDraw == null`), so each moving frame redraws all 6 faces of all 8–9 lamps. Override `UpdateShadowsOverride` in `LampLight` to skip the null pass.
2. ✔ **Light probe and reflection cube re-shoot every 10 cm of travel** (`FollowCar` resets the slot box each frame; the cube is AcTools' 2048 default). Keep the slot box still during a move, steer the shadows with `ShadowsCentre`, and drop the reflection cube to 512.
3. **Shadows redrawn while nothing moves**: a scripted car standing still counts as moving, the bench part, the hoist, the paint booth and WorkOpacity fades redraw shadows every frame, and the first frame of every move skips the budget.

Shadows: no texel snapping on the sun cascades (edges shimmer as the centre slides), the diner's lot centre slides with the car, car AO planes rock with the engine, and lifted parts keep stale boxes. Elsewhere: two rare race-return bugs (an earlier pending race's tow or bust messages leak into this race's tow logic), and the `max_rpm` bounds miss `add`/`times`.

## BUGS (must fix)

- **[BUG] Street Corsa/Controls/StreetViewport3D.cs:434** — `PlaceRival` moves the rival's slot through `CarBodyRock.Apply` every frame but never calls `FollowCar`/`RefreshShadows`. Its shadow is redrawn only when the player's idle rock crosses a lean step, so it lags behind or stays where the car loaded. Its box (used for frustum culling at actools RenderableList.cs:170) is not updated either. *Plausible* (depends on how often the player's rock crosses a step). **Fix:** refresh shadows for the rival at a throttled rate (10 Hz) and update its box every few metres.
- **[BUG] Street Corsa/Controls/GarageRenderer.cs:192-208 (AcTools ShadowsDirectional.cs Split.LookAt)** — The sun's cascades centre on the raw car-box centre (or `ShadowsCentre`), with no snapping to whole texels. While the centre slides (drive-in, the lift going up, the diner's moving centre) every cascade's texel grid slides with it, so shadow edges crawl and shimmer. That covers static scenery too, not just the moving car. **Fix:** in the `UpdateShadows` override, put the centre into light space, snap it to the coarsest cascade's texel (`Splits[^1].Size / ShadowMapSize`, about 2.4 cm), and pass the snapped point to `base.UpdateShadows`.
- **[BUG] Street Corsa/Controls/DinerViewport3D.cs:197-224** — `LotShadowsCentre` is the middle of the player's car and the occupied bays, and it is worked out again on every frame the car drives (DriveInViewport3D.cs:460-463). The frustum slides at half the car's speed, so the parked cars' shadows shimmer even though those cars never move. The sharpest cascade (car-size wide) centred about 10 m from the player's car also covers neither the player's car nor the bays, so it is wasted. **Fix:** centre on the bays only (they are static) and snap that centre, or re-centre only when the car crosses `NearTheLot`.
- **[BUG] Street Corsa/Controls/D3DViewportBase.cs:1063,1217** — `KeepShadowsOnFloor` undoes only the stance (`StanceLeanOf(_rock, stance)`) and the lift. `CarBodyRock.Apply` leans the whole car node (CarBodyRock.cs:199), `_shadows` included, with the engine rock, the bumps and the drive's dive and squat. So the body and tyre AO planes roll, pitch and heave with an idling engine (up to `BumpHeave` 8 mm) and dip into or float above the floor (*Plausible* visible flicker at the tyre contacts). **Fix:** set only `shadows.LocalMatrix = down * Matrix.Invert(_rock.LeanOf(pose))` each applied frame. That is one matrix; the costly node walk stays behind the change check.
- **[BUG] Street Corsa/Controls/GarageRenderer.cs:245-265** — `BodyLean` moves `_prop` and `_candidates` but never calls `UpdateBoundingBox()`. That includes the lift translation from D3DViewportBase.cs:1220, up to about 1.8 m. `Droop` refreshes their boxes only up to `LiftLayout.WheelDrop` (0.1 m). `RenderableList.Draw` culls by the stale box (AcTools RenderableList.cs:170), so on a raised car the mounted parts can drop out of a lamp face's or cascade's shadow pass, or out of the main view when it is seen from under the lift (*Plausible*). **Fix:** call `UpdateBoundingBox()` on both when the translation part of `value` changes (the lift), not on every lean.
- ✔ **[BUG] Street Corsa/Screens/RaceLoading/RaceLoadingScreenViewModel.cs:163** — *Plausible* (needs an earlier race left pending): `messages` holds the earlier race's messages too, because the launcher merges `run.EarlierMessages` into `result.PlayerMessages` (AssettoCorsaLauncher.cs:653/811). So an earlier crash, or an earlier police bust (`TowedToGarage = pursuit.PlayerBusted`, RaceResultProcessor.cs:506), marks this race's car `Towed`, sends the player home, and stops a fit car from driving in. **Fix:** have the launcher return the earlier race's messages apart from this race's (or tag each message with its race), and read `TowedToGarage` only from this race's.
- ✔ **[BUG] Street Corsa/Screens/RaceLoading/RaceLoadingScreenViewModel.cs:295** — *Plausible*, same cause: `PayTows` finds the first `TowedToGarage` message, which can be the earlier race's crash or bust, and charges a tow for this race's (not impounded) car. This breaks the method's own rule "Never the police's" when the bust came from the earlier pending race. **Fix:** look only at this race's messages (as above), and skip a message that comes from a bust.
- **[BUG] Street Corsa/Parts/Logic/PartFigures.cs:331** — The new `max_rpm`/`float_rpm` bounds (`LeastSpeed`..`MostSpeed`) apply only to `FigureOp.Set`. An `add` or `times` figure still combines to any value (`Combine` at :284). A large `add` makes the engine refuse to run ("revs past any", EngineDyno.cs:259). A negative `add` below `LeastSpeed` makes the part's speed silently count as none, so the engine has no limit from it (EngineAssembly.LowerMaxRpm, EngineDyno.cs:208). *Plausible* (a mod's figures). **Fix:** clamp or reject the combined `max_rpm`/`float_rpm` in `Apply` against the same bounds, and tell the problem once.
- **[BUG] installer/pack-mod.ps1:84** — *Plausible*: the trailing-comma regex runs over the code from `Read-ModJsonCode`, which still includes string contents. A mod.json value containing `", }"` or `",]"` is changed before the read-back check at :176, so the check can pass or fail on text the game reads differently. The shipped file itself is unaffected. **Fix:** drop trailing commas inside the same string-aware scan (skip a `,` whose next non-space code character is `}` or `]`) instead of using a regex.
