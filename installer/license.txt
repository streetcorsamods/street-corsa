Street Corsa

Street Corsa is a fan-made game that runs on top of Assetto Corsa, which you need to own and have installed. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Kunos Simulazioni or 505 Games. Before each race it changes the data of the car you race in your Assetto Corsa install and puts it back afterwards; it also installs its race modes for Custom Shaders Patch there. Uninstalling Street Corsa takes those modes out again. Your saves and settings stay in %AppData%\StreetCorsa.
